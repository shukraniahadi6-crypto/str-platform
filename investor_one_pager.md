# Investor One-Pager: Shuk Trash Removal (STR)
**Tagline:** The DoorDash of Trash Pickup

---

## 1. Problem
Millions of residents—especially the elderly, people with mobility limitations, busy professionals, and apartment tenants—struggle to physically move their trash carts or heavy waste bins to the curb for municipal pickup days. Missed pickups lead to HOA fines, foul odors, pest infestations, and property degradation.

## 2. Solution
An on-demand gig-economy platform connecting customers who need trash bins placed at the curb with independent local haulers (couriers) who perform the physical labor. The system handles automated dispatch, live navigation, proof of work, and payments.

## 3. Market Opportunity
* **128 Million** households in the U.S.
* **30 Million** elderly or mobility-limited residents needing assistance.
* **20 Million** apartment/condo units with restricted trash chutes or distant bins.
* High adoption rates for convenient household chore outsourcing (Instacart, TaskRabbit).

## 4. Business Model
* **Service Fees:** Flat on-demand fees starting at $30 per pickup.
* **Subscription Models:** Weekly recurring curbside placements starting at $49/month.
* **Neighborhood Batch Discounts:** Direct 25% savings for users who batch routes together, lowering truck emissions.
* **Add-Ons:** Special disposal fees, priority express windows, and upcycling handling.

## 5. Traction Plan
1. **Pilot Launch:** Cedar Rapids, IA.
2. **Expansion Area:** Iowa City & Corridor region.
3. **Statewide Scaling:** Expansion across Iowa urban centers.
4. **Commercial Phase:** Offering dumpster-management services for residential property managers.

## 6. Financial Structure & Owner Payouts
* **Gross Margins:** 45% retained gross profit margin on platform fees.
* **Owner/Founder Profit Share:** 45% of platform net profit allocated directly to the founder (**Shukrani Ahadi**).
* **Funding Ask:** $150,000 for development, marketing expansion, and insurance/compliance licensing.
