# Marketing & Launch Plan: Shuk Trash Removal (STR)
**Subject:** Customer Acquisition Channels and Growth Strategy

---

## 1. Target Audience Segmentation
* **Elderly & Disabled Residents:** High-need demographic unable to transport heavy rollout bins to curbs.
* **Busy Urban Professionals:** Shift workers and commuting professionals seeking convenience.
* **Condo & Apartment Dwellers:** Tenants whose building garbage chutes are far away or frequently blocked.
* **Property Managers & HOAs:** Commercial partners looking to enforce clean curbside aesthetics and avoid bin issues.

## 2. Marketing Channels & Budgets

| Channel | Tactics | Budget Allocation |
|---|---|---|
| **Hyperlocal Social Ads** | Facebook groups, Nextdoor community ads, Instagram Reels displaying before/after pickups. | 40% |
| **Direct Mail Flyer Drops** | Physical cards delivered directly to targeted zip codes (focused on older/suburban neighborhoods). | 25% |
| **Search Engine Marketing** | Google Ads targeting local intent keywords (e.g. "trash helper near me", "haul junk Cedar Rapids"). | 20% |
| **Property Partnerships** | B2B outreach offering building-wide subscriptions for apartment complexes. | 15% |

## 3. Brand & Message Strategy
* **Tagline:** "Let Shuk take it to the curb."
* **Core Value Props:**
  * No heavy lifting required.
  * Reliable, DoorDash-style real-time tracking.
  * Eco-conscious: 25% emissions discount for route-batching and direct Upcycling/Donation support.
* **App Launch Promo:** "First curbside pickup is completely free."

## 4. Launch Timeline
* **Month 1 (Soft Launch):** Pilot release in Cedar Rapids, targeting three specific neighborhoods. Recruited 15 certified haulers.
* **Month 3 (Expansion):** Enter Iowa City corridor, roll out subscription rollout packages.
* **Month 6 (Statewide):** Scale API dispatch in Des Moines and Davenport metro regions.
