# STR Platform — Revenue Model

## 2. Per-Pickup Commissions

**Model:** STR retains 25–30% of each job fee paid by the customer.

- Blended take rate: **27.5%**
- Remainder (70%) paid to courier; 2.5% to payment processing (Stripe)
- Commission rate is non-negotiable for standard jobs; B2B volume jobs negotiated separately

### Commission Scaling

| Monthly GMV Tier | Take Rate | Notes |
|-----------------|----------|-------|
| $0 – $100K | 28.5% | Launch markets, higher overhead |
| $100K – $500K | 27.5% | Standard rate |
| $500K+ | 26.0% | Scale efficiency passes partial savings to couriers |

### GMV → Net Revenue Bridge (Year 2 projection)

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Monthly GMV (avg) | $2.5M |
| Annual GMV | $30M |
| Take rate | 27.5% |
| Gross commission revenue | $8.25M |
| Less: payment processing (2.5% of GMV) | ($750K) |
| **Net commission revenue** | **$7.5M** |

## 4. Eco-Credit Marketplace

**Model:** STR issues "EcoCredits" to customers for verified waste diversion (recycling, composting, e-waste). Credits are redeemable for discounts, donated to environmental causes, or sold on the STR marketplace.

### Credit Economics

| Event | Credits Earned |
|-------|---------------|
| Verified recycling pickup (per lb diverted) | 2 credits/lb |
| Composting pickup (per bin) | 50 credits |
| E-waste certified disposal | 150 credits |
| Referral resulting in eco-certified job | 100 credits |

| Redemption Option | Credits Required | STR Revenue Impact |
|-----------------|-----------------|-------------------|
| $5 discount on next job | 200 credits | −$5 cost offset by increased retention |
| Donate to tree-planting partner | 100 credits | $0.50 pass-through to charity partner |
| Sell on marketplace (peer-to-peer) | Market price | STR takes 15% of marketplace transaction |

**Revenue from marketplace:** 15% spread on all credit trades + B2B companies purchasing credits for ESG offsets.

## 5. B2B Partnerships

### Revenue Types

| Partnership Type | Revenue Model | Year 2 Est. |
|----------------|--------------|------------|
| Municipal overflow contracts | Per-job fee + monthly SLA fee | $650K |
| Property management integrations | Monthly licensing fee ($199–$999/property) | $480K |
| Recycling center inbound volume deals | Per-ton payment for sorted recyclable delivery | $320K |
| Corporate sustainability programs | Annual subscription for CO₂ offset reporting | $350K |
| **Total B2B Year 2** | | **$1.8M** |

### Municipal Contract Structure

- STR bids as a certified overflow hauler for city sanitation departments
- Fixed per-pickup fee paid by city when municipal truck is unavailable
- Data-sharing agreement provides route efficiency data to city
- Contract terms: 12–24 months with renewal option
