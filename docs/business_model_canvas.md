# STR Platform — Business Model Canvas

## Overview

STR (Sustainable Trash Removal) is an on-demand marketplace that connects residential and commercial customers with vetted, eco-friendly couriers for household and small-business trash pickup and responsible disposal.

---

## 1. Customer Segments

### Residential
- Homeowners generating excess or bulky waste not covered by standard municipal pickup
- Renters in multi-unit buildings with limited or inconvenient shared trash facilities
- Seniors and mobility-impaired individuals who cannot transport trash to collection points
- Short-term rental (Airbnb/VRBO) hosts needing rapid turnover cleanup

### Commercial (Small Business)
- Restaurants and food-service businesses with daily organic waste overflow
- Retail shops needing regular cardboard/packaging removal
- Offices with electronics or confidential document disposal needs

### Corporate / B2B
- Property management companies (apartment complexes, HOAs)
- Event organizers (post-event cleanup)
- Municipalities and city sanitation departments seeking surge-capacity partnerships
- Recycling centers seeking inbound material volume

---

## 2. Value Propositions

| Segment | Value Proposition |
|---------|------------------|
| Residential | Convenient, same-day or scheduled trash removal from doorstep; no heavy lifting |
| Commercial | Reliable daily/weekly pickups; compliance with local waste regulations |
| Corporate/B2B | Volume pricing, dedicated account management, sustainability reporting |
| Couriers | Flexible earning opportunities; route optimization; transparent payouts |

**Core differentiators:**
- **Eco-first routing** — couriers are trained and incentivized to sort recyclables and compostables
- **Eco-credit rewards** — customers earn credits redeemable for discounts when they divert waste from landfill
- **Transparent impact tracking** — real-time dashboard showing CO₂ avoided, recycling rate, and landfill diversion
- **Verified couriers** — background-checked, vehicle-inspected, eco-certified

## 9. Cost Structure

| Category | Est. % of Operating Costs |
|----------|--------------------------|
| Technology (infrastructure, development) | 28% |
| Marketing & Customer Acquisition | 22% |
| Courier Support & Operations | 18% |
| G&A (legal, finance, HR) | 15% |
| Customer Support | 10% |
| Insurance & Compliance | 7% |

**Cost drivers:**
- Engineering talent (highest single cost)
- Paid acquisition (Google, Meta ads) in new markets
- Courier incentive programs during market launch

**Economies of scale:**
- Technology costs grow sub-linearly with volume
- Route density reduces per-pickup courier cost
- B2B contracts provide predictable revenue with lower acquisition cost per dollar

## 10. Key Metrics & KPIs

### Growth KPIs
- Monthly Active Customers (MAC)
- Monthly Active Couriers (MAU — courier)
- Gross Merchandise Value (GMV)
- Number of jobs completed per month
- Market penetration rate (cities live)

### Financial KPIs
- Net Revenue (take rate × GMV)
- Gross Margin
- Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
- Lifetime Value (LTV)
- LTV:CAC ratio (target ≥ 3:1)
- Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) from subscriptions

### Operational KPIs
- Pickup completion rate (target > 97%)
- Average courier response time (target < 15 min)
- Customer satisfaction score / NPS (target > 50)
- Courier utilization rate
- Dispute rate (target < 2%)

### Sustainability KPIs
- Tons of waste diverted from landfill per month
- Recycling rate (% of collected waste recycled)
- CO₂ emissions avoided
- Eco-credits issued vs. redeemed
