# STR Platform — Investor Pitch Summary

## Slide 1: Problem Statement

### The Waste Problem Nobody's Solving Well

- The average U.S. household generates **4.4 lbs of trash per day** — yet municipal pickup occurs only **1–2 times per week**
- **47% of Americans** say they have waste they cannot easily dispose of: bulky items, electronics, organic waste, recycling overflow
- Existing solutions are broken:
  - **Municipal programs** → rigid schedules, no bulk pickup, zero eco-accountability
  - **Private haulers** → expensive ($150–$400/visit), slow to book, cash-only, no transparency
  - **DIY disposal** → time-consuming, often results in illegal dumping or landfill contribution
- **$12B** in junk/overflow removal happens annually in the U.S. — with no dominant, tech-enabled, eco-conscious player

> "The last category that gig-economy disruption hasn't touched is the stuff under your feet."

---

## Slide 2: Solution

### STR: On-Demand Trash Removal, Eco-Certified

STR is a two-sided marketplace connecting customers with **vetted, eco-trained couriers** for convenient, responsible waste pickup.

**How it works:**
1. Customer opens the STR app and requests a pickup (30-second booking)
2. A verified local courier accepts and arrives (average < 20 minutes)
3. Waste is collected and routed to the appropriate facility (recycling, compost, certified disposal)
4. Customer receives an eco-impact card showing their environmental contribution
5. Courier is paid same-day; STR retains a 27.5% platform fee

**Key differentiators:**
- ✅ Eco-routing — every job sorts and routes waste responsibly
- ✅ Eco-credits — customers earn rewards for verified recycling/composting
- ✅ Verified couriers — background-checked, vehicle-inspected, eco-certified
- ✅ Transparent pricing — no surprise fees; dynamic pricing with clear communication
- ✅ Subscription model — recurring revenue from high-frequency users
