# STR Platform — Growth & Retention Strategy

## 1. Growth Philosophy

STR grows through **density-first expansion** — launching in a single city, achieving supply-demand balance, then expanding to adjacent markets. This avoids the "thin marketplace" problem where too few couriers or customers make the platform unreliable.

**Growth Flywheel:**
```
More customers → More job volume → Better courier earnings
→ More couriers join → Faster response times → Better customer experience
→ More reviews & referrals → More customers
```

## 3. Viral Loops

### Loop 1: Eco-Impact Social Sharing
- After each pickup, customer receives a personalized eco-impact card
- Card shows: pounds diverted from landfill, CO₂ saved, equivalent trees planted
- One-tap share to Instagram Stories, WhatsApp, LinkedIn
- Share includes referral link → new user sign-up with $5 credit

### Loop 2: Neighbor Effect
- When a courier is active on a street, nearby users receive a push notification: "A courier is 2 minutes away — add a pickup for $X"
- Converts passive awareness into immediate bookings
- Increases courier utilization and creates neighborhood word-of-mouth

### Loop 3: Subscription Upgrade Loop
- Free users who complete 3 jobs receive in-app offer: "You've saved $X with STR — unlock Pro for unlimited pickups"
- Subscription users who refer 3 friends get 1 free month
- Subscription churn triggers a 30-day win-back campaign with personalized discount

## 4. Courier Acquisition & Supply Growth

| Channel | Tactic |
|---------|--------|
| Craigslist & Facebook gigs groups | "Earn $500–$800/week — flexible hours" ads |
| Gig worker platforms (DoorDash off-peak) | Target drivers with utilization gaps |
| Community colleges & trade schools | Flyer campaigns; STR as supplemental income |
| Social media (TikTok/YouTube) | "Day in the life of an STR courier" content |
| Existing courier referrals | $75 bonus per referred courier completing 20 jobs |

### Supply-Side Incentives

- **Launch bonus:** First 50 couriers in a new city earn +$5/job for first 60 days
- **Utilization guarantee:** Couriers guaranteed minimum 8 jobs/day during launch or STR supplements
- **Rating protection:** New couriers get 30-day "probation" window; early low ratings not counted

## 5. Retention Mechanics

### Customer Retention

| Mechanic | Trigger | Action |
|---------|---------|--------|
| **Post-pickup eco-card** | Every completed job | Personalized sustainability impact; share prompt |
| **Subscription nudge** | After 2nd job | In-app: "Save 20% with Pro — your next job is covered" |
| **Win-back campaign** | No booking in 45 days | Push + email: "We miss you — $5 off your next pickup" |
| **Eco-credit expiry warning** | Credits expiring in 14 days | Push notification driving re-engagement |
| **Anniversary reward** | 6-month customer milestone | Surprise discount or free premium feature |
| **Seasonal reminders** | Spring cleaning, move-out season (May/August) | Targeted push campaigns |

### Courier Retention

| Mechanic | Trigger | Action |
|---------|---------|--------|
| **Earnings dashboard** | Weekly summary | Push: "You earned $X this week — top 15% of couriers!" |
| **Streak bonus** | 10+ jobs with no cancellation | $25 cash bonus |
| **Level-up program** | Complete 100 jobs → "Silver Courier" | Higher job priority, +$0.50/job rate |
| **Community** | Monthly courier meetups | Local events; feedback collection |
| **Insurance support** | Courier milestone | STR subsidizes liability insurance after 200 jobs |

## 6. Referral Program

### Customer Referral

| Action | Referrer Reward | Referred User Reward |
|--------|----------------|---------------------|
| Refer a friend who completes first job | $10 EcoCredit | $5 off first job |
| Refer a Pro subscriber | $15 EcoCredit | First month 50% off |
| Refer a business (Premium/Enterprise) | $75 cash | First month free |

### Referral Mechanics
- Unique shareable link + QR code generated per user
- Referral tracked via cookie + phone number confirmation
- Credited immediately upon referred user's first completed job
- Cap: No limit on residential referrals; B2B referrals verified by account team
