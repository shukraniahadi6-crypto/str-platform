# Vendor Onboarding Guide

## Overview

This guide walks customers through setting up their STR Platform account, requesting their first pickup, and using the platform's eco features.

## Step 1: Create an Account

1. Go to `https://app.strplatform.com/signup`
2. Enter your name, email, and password
3. Select **Customer** as your role
4. Verify your email via the confirmation link

## Step 2: Add a Payment Method

1. In your dashboard, go to **Settings → Payment Methods**
2. Click **Add Card** (powered by Stripe — card details never touch STR servers)
3. Enter your card details and save

## Step 3: Request a Pickup

1. Click **New Pickup Request** on your dashboard
2. **Take 1–3 photos** of your waste pile
3. Upload the photos to receive an **AI-generated estimate** with itemized pricing
4. Review the estimate, then select a pickup window:
   - **Express** (within 60 minutes, +$10 fee)
   - **Scheduled** (2-hour window on a future date, discounted)
5. Toggle **Upcycle Mode** for any items you'd like donated instead of disposed
6. Check if a **Neighborhood Batch** is available for additional savings
7. Confirm and pay

## Step 4: Track Your Hauler

Once a hauler accepts your job:
- Watch the **live map** with real-time hauler location and ETA
- Receive SMS/push notifications on status changes
- Chat with your hauler via in-app messaging

## Step 5: Review Your Green Impact

After job completion:
- View your **Green Impact Card** (CO2 saved, landfill diversion %, trees equivalent)
- Share your eco-receipt on social media
- Download a tax receipt for donated items

## Joining a Neighborhood Batch

If you receive a push notification saying a neighbor has booked a pickup:

1. Tap the notification or open the **Batches** tab
2. View the batch details (scheduled time, discount %)
3. Tap **Join This Run** to add your items at the discounted rate
