# Courier Training Guide: Shuk Trash Removal (STR)
**Subject:** Professional Standards, Safety, and Dispatch Operations

---

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to Shuk Trash Removal! As an independent courier (hauler), you are the face of STR. This guide outlines standard operating procedures, safety measures, and app flows to ensure you maximize earnings while providing top-tier service.

## 2. Mandatory Equipment
To run pick-ups on the STR platform, you must possess and carry:
* **Heavy-Duty Work Gloves:** For safety handling wood, metal, or raw plastic.
* **Safety Glasses:** For protection during loading/unloading tasks.
* **Flashlight / Headlamp:** Mandatory for late-evening or early-morning runs.
* **Heavy-Duty Trash Bags:** 55-gallon contractor bags for consolidation.
* **STR App-Enabled Smartphone:** Mounted in your vehicle for live tracking and GPS telemetry.

## 3. Core Job Flow
1. **Receive Offer Ping:** Review the upfront pay, distance, volume ($yd^3$), and waste category. You have 30 seconds to accept or decline.
2. **Navigate to Site:** Lock in GPS routing and travel safely to the customer location.
3. **Conduct Visual Inspection:** Match the garbage heap with the photo provided by the customer.
4. **Load & Transport:** Carefully load the materials into your vehicle. Keep load weight balanced.
5. **Curb or Disposal Delivery:** Deposit municipal waste at the designated curbside point, or travel to the listed eco-station/recycling center.
6. **Upload Completion Photo:** Take a clean photo showing the curb/eco-station with bins neatly aligned.
7. **Complete & Collect Earnings:** Submit the photo in-app to trigger the ledger entry and unlock your payout.

## 4. Platform Safety Regulations
> [!WARNING]
> **Prohibited Hazard Materials:** Under no circumstances should you accept, load, or transport medical waste, loose syringes, explosive canisters, or unidentified raw chemical fluids.
* **SDS Cases:** If a job has been flagged as containing Class 3 flammable liquids (e.g. automotive oil or solvents), ensure you have reviewed the active Safety Data Sheet protocols in-app before beginning.
* **No Home Entry:** Never enter a customer's residence or backyard unless specifically authorized for exterior-only gate access. All service operations must remain outside.

## 5. Maximizing Earnings & Tips
* **Eco Academy Certifications:** Pass the heavy-lifting, SDS, and upcycling quizzes in-app to qualify for higher-tier service fees.
* **Upcycling (Karma Bonuses):** Identify reusable items (wood, metal, electronics). Diverting these to local donation hubs yields an extra $5.00 platform "Karma Bonus" per job.

## 6. Courier Tax & Classification
### 6.1 Independent Contractor Status (1099-NEC)
All couriers operating on the STR Platform are classified as independent contractors (1099-NEC), not employees. This classification determines how earnings, taxes, and reporting obligations are handled.

### 6.2 Tax Withholding Policy
The STR Platform does not withhold taxes from courier payouts. Couriers receive their full earnings, and are solely responsible for calculating, reporting, and paying any taxes owed.

### 6.3 Courier Tax Obligations
Couriers must manage and pay their own:
* Federal income tax
* State income tax
* Local income tax (if applicable)
* Self-employment tax (Social Security + Medicare)
* Quarterly estimated tax payments (if required by the IRS)

### 6.4 Year-End Reporting
If a courier earns $600 or more in a calendar year, the STR Platform will issue a Form 1099-NEC reflecting total earnings. Couriers must use this form when filing their annual tax return.

### 6.5 Expense Deductions
Couriers may be eligible to deduct business-related expenses, including but not limited to:
* Mileage and vehicle costs
* Equipment (bags, gloves, containers)
* Phone/data usage
* Safety gear
* Other operational expenses necessary for performing courier duties

Couriers are responsible for maintaining accurate records of all deductible expenses.

### 6.6 Division of Responsibilities
**The STR Platform is responsible for:**
* Providing accurate annual earnings statements
* Issuing 1099-NEC forms when required
* Maintaining compliance with IRS contractor reporting rules

**The STR Platform is NOT responsible for:**
* Calculating courier taxes
* Providing tax advice
* Managing courier expense records
* Paying employer-side payroll taxes (since couriers are not employees)
